Machine-Processed News & Sentiment Engine

How raw headlines get aggregated, scored and catalog-tagged before they reach the Story Feed.

Source hierarchy

SEC EDGAR filings rank highest (primary, verifiable), then tier-1 wires (Bloomberg, Reuters, Benzinga), then corporate press releases — each headline is weighted by source tier before clustering.

NLP scoring

Every headline gets a polarity score from -1 (bearish) to +1 (bullish), plus a rolling 24-hour sentiment momentum reading and a check for abnormal headline-volume spikes on a symbol.

Catalyst tagging

Stories are tagged by event type — M&A, management turnover, regulatory action, product launch — so the feed can be filtered by what kind of catalyst is driving the move.

Sources & Coverage

Where the news comes from, and how raw headlines become the stories on this dashboard.

Alpaca News API

Same paper-account credentials used for trading. Queried per symbol with a 20-hour lookback, deduped by article ID.

Benzinga

Primary newswire behind Alpaca's feed — real-time headlines, often before summaries are written.

Read-only

This dashboard never places trades or touches bot state — it only reads news and the Alpaca market clock.

1

Fetch

fetch_news.py pulls recent articles for SPY, QQQ and DIA, tags each as S&P 500 / Nasdaq 100 relevant, and flags macro-keyword matches.

2

Cluster & analyze

Related headlines are grouped into a single story, with a plain-English read on likely direction for each index — not just a headline dump.

3

Publish

The dashboard is rebuilt and republished to the same link, so it's always the latest read without a new URL to track down.
